Privacy Policy

FocusOnIt (专注猫猫) · Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FocusOnIt ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our iOS application (the "App"). The App is designed to work on your device. We do not operate user accounts, cloud sync, or advertising tracking in the current version.

1. Information We Process

  • Focus records and settings — session history, tags, daily goals, pet companion state, shop items, and preferences are stored locally on your device (including an App Group container used by widgets and Live Activities).
  • Screen Time selections — if you enable Focus Guard, Apple’s FamilyControls / ManagedSettings APIs keep the selected apps and restrictions on your device. We do not receive a list of your apps on our servers.
  • Photos or camera images — only if you choose “custom pet” and pick a photo. The image is processed and stored on your device for display in the App. We do not upload it.
  • Subscription and purchase status — processed by Apple through App Store / StoreKit. We receive entitlement information needed to unlock VIP features or credit Lucky Points. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Notifications — if you grant permission, the App schedules local reminders (for example, focus complete, break complete, or a daily reminder). We do not send promotional push campaigns in the current version.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require registration or login.
  • We do not operate our own backend servers to collect or store your focus history, photos, or pet data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or App Tracking Transparency tracking.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions. You can manage or revoke them in iOS Settings at any time.

  • Screen Time — optional. Used only for Focus Guard (temporarily putting distracting apps aside, or keeping only apps you allow during a session).
  • Notifications — optional. Used for local reminders you enable.
  • Camera — optional. Used only if you take a photo for a custom pet companion.
  • Photo Library — optional. Used only if you choose an existing photo for a custom pet companion.

Focus, pet companionship, and basic statistics still work if you decline optional permissions. Focus Guard requires Screen Time authorization.

4. Subscriptions, Points, and Payments

VIP membership and Lucky Point packs are billed through your Apple ID using Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment according to Apple’s privacy policy. We receive purchase / subscription status needed to provide the corresponding in-App benefits.

Current auto-renewing VIP products include:

  • Weekly VIP (com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.vip.weekly)
  • Monthly VIP (com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.vip.monthly)
  • Yearly VIP (com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.vip.yearly)

Current consumable Lucky Point packs include:

  • com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.points.60
  • com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.points.new180
  • com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.points.680
  • com.TextBeta.www.FocusOnIt.points.1280

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple system services, including App Store / StoreKit, Screen Time (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity), User Notifications, widgets, and Live Activities. Apple’s handling of data is governed by Apple’s policies. When you use iOS share sheets, those actions are handled by Apple’s system services under your control.

6. Data Retention

Focus records, settings, pet data, and custom photos remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Subscription and purchase records are managed by Apple.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. Because most data stays on your device, you should also protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

9. International Users

If you use the App outside your home country, information is processed on your device and through Apple services in accordance with applicable law. Availability may vary by App Store country or region.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

隐私政策

FocusOnIt（专注猫猫）· 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 28 日

本隐私政策说明 FocusOnIt（以下简称「我们」）在你使用 iOS 应用（以下简称「本应用」）时如何处理相关信息。 本应用以设备本地使用为主，当前版本不提供账号体系、云端同步或广告追踪。

1. 我们处理的信息

  • 专注记录与设置：会话历史、标签、每日目标、宠物状态、小店物品和偏好保存在你的设备本地（含小组件与实时活动使用的 App Group 容器）。
  • 屏幕时间选择：若你开启专注守护，Apple 的 FamilyControls / ManagedSettings 会在设备上保存所选 App 与限制；我们不会把你的 App 列表上传到自有服务器。
  • 照片或相机图像：仅在你选择「自定义宠物」并选取照片时处理，图像保存在设备本地用于展示，我们不会上传。
  • 订阅与购买状态：通过 Apple App Store / StoreKit 处理。我们仅接收用于解锁 VIP 权益或发放幸运积分的购买状态，不会获取完整支付卡信息。
  • 通知：在你授权后，本应用会安排本地提醒（如专注完成、休息结束、每日提醒）。当前版本不发送促销推送。

2. 我们不收集的信息

  • 当前版本不要求注册或登录。
  • 我们不运营后端服务器来收集或存储你的专注历史、照片或宠物数据。
  • 我们不使用第三方分析、广告 SDK，也不进行 App Tracking Transparency 追踪。
  • 我们不出售你的个人信息。

3. 设备权限

本应用可能请求以下权限，你可随时在 iOS「设置」中管理或撤销：

  • 屏幕时间（可选）：仅用于专注守护（暂时放下分心 App，或只保留专注时可用的 App）。
  • 通知（可选）：用于你开启的本地提醒。
  • 相机（可选）：仅在为自定义宠物拍照时使用。
  • 相册（可选）：仅在从相册选择自定义宠物照片时使用。

拒绝可选权限后，专注计时、宠物陪伴和基础统计仍可使用。专注守护需要屏幕时间授权。
