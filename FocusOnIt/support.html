Welcome to FocusOnIt support. If you need help with focus sessions, Screen Time / Focus Guard, VIP membership, Lucky Points, or permissions, please review the information below or contact us directly.

Contact Email mimengtechcto@163.com

We usually reply within 1–3 business days. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.

1. Getting Started

  • Focus timer — start a countdown or count-up session with a tag. Simple focus is always free.
  • Pet companion — your cat stays with you during sessions and reflects how the focus went.
  • Challenge intensity — choose how strictly leaving the App counts as an interruption (Easy / Standard / Hard / Hell). Hard and Hell require VIP.
  • Focus Guard — optional Screen Time restriction: leave distracting apps aside, or keep only apps you allow during a session. Core blocking is free.
  • Stats — today’s total and success count are available to everyone; detailed timeline insights require VIP.

2. Permissions

If a feature is unavailable, check iOS Settings and make sure the required permission is enabled for FocusOnIt:

  • Screen Time — required for Focus Guard
  • Notifications — optional, used for local reminders (focus complete, break complete, daily reminder)
  • Camera / Photo Library — optional, used only for a custom pet photo

You can still focus, keep the default pet, and use basic stats without optional permissions.

3. FocusOnIt VIP

While a VIP subscription is active: Hard / Hell modes, detailed stats and timeline, the shop wardrobe without redeeming, and 10× Lucky Points on recharge packs. Simple focus stays free.

  • Weekly VIP — auto-renewing every week
  • Yearly VIP — auto-renewing every year (monthly SKU may exist for StoreKit compatibility; the App highlights weekly and yearly)
  • Payment is processed by Apple through your Apple ID
  • Use Restore in the App if you changed devices or reinstalled
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions

4. Lucky Points

  • You can earn points by focusing and unlocking achievements.
  • You can also buy consumable point packs in the Points Mall (processed by Apple).
  • VIP members receive 10× points when purchasing recharge packs.
  • Items permanently redeemed with points stay after VIP expires; items only used via VIP free-wear return to default when the subscription ends.

5. Common Issues

  • Focus Guard does not block apps — grant Screen Time / Family Controls permission, pick the apps to set aside or allow, then start a session with Guard enabled.
  • Subscription prices not loading — connect to the internet and sign in with a valid Apple ID. New products may take time to appear in sandbox or App Store.
  • Restore did not work — use the same Apple ID that made the purchase, then try Restore on the membership page.
  • Hard / Hell mode locked — these challenge levels require an active VIP subscription. Easy / Standard remain available.
  • Custom pet photo missing — allow Camera or Photo Library access, then choose the photo again. Custom photos stay on this device only.
  • Notifications not arriving — enable Notifications for FocusOnIt in iOS Settings, and turn on the reminder types you want in the App.

6. Data & Privacy

FocusOnIt is designed to work on your device. We do not operate user accounts or cloud sync in the current version. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.

我们通常会在 1–3 个工作日内回复。来信请注明设备型号、iOS 版本，以及问题的简要描述。

1. 功能介绍

  • 专注计时：用标签开始倒计时或正计时。简单专注始终免费。
  • 宠物陪伴：小黑猫会陪你完成这一轮，并用状态反馈结果。
  • 挑战强度：选择离开 App 严不严（简单 / 标准 / 困难 / 地狱）。困难与地狱需 VIP。
  • 专注守护：可选的屏幕时间限制——暂时放下分心 App，或只保留专注时可用的 App。核心拦截免费。
  • 统计：今日合计与成功次数对所有人开放；时间线详情需 VIP。

2. 权限说明

若功能无法使用，请在 iOS「设置」中检查 FocusOnIt 的相关权限是否已开启：

  • 屏幕时间：专注守护必需
  • 通知：可选，用于本地提醒（专注完成、休息结束、每日提醒）
  • 相机 / 相册：可选，仅用于自定义宠物照片

不授予可选权限时，仍可专注、使用默认宠物和基础统计。

3. FocusOnIt VIP

订阅有效期内：困难 / 地狱模式、统计与时间线详情、小店免兑换换装，以及充值积分 10 倍到账。简单专注始终免费。

  • 周会员：按周自动续订
  • 年会员：按年自动续订（月卡可能存在于 StoreKit 兼容，App 内主要展示周卡与年卡）
  • 付款由 Apple 通过 Apple ID 处理
  • 换机或重装后，可在 App 内点击「恢复」
  • 可在 iOS「设置 > Apple ID > 订阅」中管理或取消

4. 幸运积分

  • 可通过专注与成就获得积分。
  • 也可在积分商城购买消耗型积分包（由 Apple 处理付款）。
  • VIP 会员购买充值包时积分为 10 倍到账。
  • 用积分永久兑换的物品在退订后仍可使用；仅因 VIP 免兑换而穿戴的物品会在订阅结束后回退。

5. 常见问题

  • 专注守护没有挡住 App：请授予屏幕时间权限，选好暂时放下或允许的 App，再在开启守护的情况下开始专注。
  • 订阅价格加载不出来：请确认网络正常且已登录 Apple ID；新商品在沙盒或 App Store 可能需要一定时间生效。
  • 恢复购买无效：请使用购买时的同一个 Apple ID，并在会员页重试恢复。
  • 困难 / 地狱模式锁定：这两档需要有效 VIP。简单 / 标准仍可使用。
  • 自定义宠物照片不见了：请允许相机或相册权限后重新选择。自定义照片只保存在本机。
  • 收不到通知：请在 iOS 设置中开启 FocusOnIt 通知，并在 App 内打开你需要的提醒类型。
