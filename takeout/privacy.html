Nibble Dash ("the app") is developed by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version: Nibble Dash has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. We do not collect your personal information, and nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Nibble Dash has no user accounts, no sign-in, no registration, and no way for you to send us data through the app. The app contains no advertising software development kits, no analytics software development kits, no crash reporting services, and no third-party trackers.

The app makes no network requests. All of its content, including every shop, dish, image, and delivery route, is fictional and ships inside the app itself.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally on your iPhone so the game can continue where you left off. This information stays in the app's private storage and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It includes:

  • Your crumb balance, crumb history, and pretend order records.
  • Garden progress, planted seeds, and unlocked crops.
  • Daily check-in streaks and rider task progress.
  • App settings such as language preference, saving goal, coupons, and meal reminder times.

If you delete the app, this information is removed from your device. We keep no copy, because we never receive one.

Health and motion data

Rider tasks in Nibble Dash are completed by walking. To count your steps, the app asks your permission to read step count data from Apple Health (HealthKit) and, when Health data is unavailable, from Core Motion.

  • Step count is read on your device only, and is used solely to advance in-app rider tasks and award in-game crumbs.
  • Step count is never transmitted off your device, never stored on any server, and never linked to any identity.
  • We never use Health or Motion data for advertising or marketing.
  • We never sell, rent, share, or disclose Health or Motion data to any third party.
  • We do not use Health or Motion data for any purpose other than the app features described above.

Granting this permission is entirely optional. You can decline it, and the rest of the app works normally. You can review or revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security, in Health > Data Access & Devices, or in Settings > Nibble Dash > Motion & Fitness.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device to tell you when a pretend order has arrived and to deliver any meal reminders you set up yourself. These notifications are generated on your device. We do not operate a push notification server and we do not receive a device token. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings > Nibble Dash > Notifications.

Live Activities and widgets

The app can display the progress of a pretend delivery in a Live Activity, on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island, and in widgets. This content is generated on your device from your local game state and is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

This version of Nibble Dash contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Crumbs are an in-game play currency. They cannot be bought, they have no monetary value, and they cannot be exchanged for money, goods, or services outside the app. No real food is ever ordered, paid for, or delivered through the app.

If we add optional purchases in a future version, they will be processed by Apple. We would not receive your payment card details, and we will update this policy before any such feature ships.

App Store data

Apple may collect its own information when you download or use the app, for example aggregated download and crash statistics. That collection is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Any reports we can view through App Store Connect are aggregated and do not identify you.

Children's privacy

Nibble Dash is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and there is nothing for a parent to request that we delete from our systems. If you have questions about your child's use of the app, contact us at the address below.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on any server, we hold no personal data about you to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control of the information stored on your own device:

  • To delete all app data, delete the app from your iPhone.
  • To stop step tracking, revoke Health or Motion permission in iOS Settings.
  • To stop notifications, turn them off in iOS Settings.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, these same facts apply. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months.
