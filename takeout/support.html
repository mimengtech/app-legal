Nibble Dash is a pretend food delivery game. Every shop, dish, coupon, fee, address, and delivery route is fictional. No real food is ever ordered, paid for, or delivered.

Frequently asked questions

Is real food actually delivered?

No. Nothing in Nibble Dash connects to a real restaurant or delivery service. The whole app is a game, and all merchants and menus are invented.

Was I charged money?

No. Nibble Dash has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Prices shown in the app are in crumbs, an in-game play currency with no monetary value. If you see a charge on your Apple account, it did not come from this app, and you should review your purchase history at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I earn crumbs?

Check in daily on the Home tab for a once-per-day reward, complete rider step tasks, and finish pretend orders and garden harvests. The seven-day check-in cycle pays 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 50, and then 80 crumbs. Missing a day resets the streak, but crumbs you have already earned are never removed.

Why does the app ask for Health or Motion permission?

Rider tasks are completed by walking, so the app reads your step count from Apple Health or, if Health is unavailable, from Core Motion. Steps are read on your device only, are used exclusively to complete in-app rider tasks, and never leave your iPhone. The permission is optional, and the rest of the app works without it.

My steps are not counting.

Check that permission is granted in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Nibble Dash, and in Settings > Nibble Dash > Motion & Fitness. Carry your iPhone while you walk, then reopen the Rider tab to refresh. Steps recorded before a rider task started are not counted toward that task.

The delivery timer seems slow. Can I skip the wait?

The wait is the point of the app, so there is no skip button and nothing you can buy to remove it. You can leave the app while you wait. The Live Activity and Dynamic Island keep the pretend delivery visible, and a notification tells you when it arrives.

I am not getting notifications.

Turn them on in Settings > Nibble Dash > Notifications, and make sure Focus modes or Scheduled Summary are not holding them back. Meal reminders also need to be enabled inside the app under Me > Meal Reminders.

The Live Activity is not showing on my Lock Screen.

Live Activities must be allowed in Settings > Nibble Dash > Live Activities. On iPhone models with a Dynamic Island, the same activity also appears there.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Open Me > Settings > Language. The app ships in American English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Is my data backed up or synced between devices?

No. All progress is stored locally on your device. There is no account, no server, and no sync. If you delete the app or move to a new iPhone without an iCloud device backup, your progress does not carry over.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes everything the app stored. We hold no copy, because we never receive one.

Does Nibble Dash help with dieting or overeating?

No. Nibble Dash is entertainment only. It is not a medical, dietary, weight-loss, or recovery product and makes no health claims. Please talk to a qualified professional about any health concern.
