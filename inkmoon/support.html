Frequently Asked Questions

Does Inkmoon require an internet connection?

No. Inkmoon’s core reading, listening, browsing, saving, and game features work offline.

How do I enable the daily poem reminder?

Open Inkmoon, go to Me, and select Daily Poem Notification. You can also manage notification permission in the iPhone Settings app.

Why can’t I hear the recitation?

Check your device volume and confirm that audio is not being routed to another Bluetooth device. Then close and reopen the poem reader and tap the Listen button again.

Where are my saved poems and preferences stored?

Saved poems and preferences are stored locally on your device. Inkmoon does not require an account or cloud service.

How do I add an Inkmoon widget?

Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen, tap Edit or the plus button, search for Inkmoon, and select a widget size.

How do I remove my Inkmoon data?

Deleting Inkmoon from your device removes its locally stored app data.
